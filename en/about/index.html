About me

(01)

I'm Tony, an art director and graphic designer based in Madrid.

Photography runs in the family. My mother has always loved it, and she was the one who gave me my first camera, around the time I graduated. Since 2017 it has been a central part of my work: I photograph and direct people, from models to content creators and public-facing profiles, and I shape the look of each shoot, from the first references to the last frame.

What I enjoy most is telling stories through images, especially in fashion and lifestyle, and that's what I look for in art direction, photography, graphic design, branded content and influencer marketing.
