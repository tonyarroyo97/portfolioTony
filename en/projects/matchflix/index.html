Concept

A conceptual rebrand of a matcha brand. The essence of the product stays the same; what changes completely is the way it is perceived.

Rebrand of a matcha brand: identity, packaging and graphic applications.

The project

The proposal takes the brand into the territory of quiet luxury: a new colour palette, more refined typography and a restrained graphic language, applied consistently across every touchpoint. The goal is a more premium, contemporary positioning, where matcha speaks the visual language of understated luxury.
