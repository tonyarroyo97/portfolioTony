Metamorfosis

Disciplines

Editorial design · Illustration · Art direction · Writing · Storytelling

(03)

Concept

My Final Degree Project, a book about endings and about closing one chapter so another can begin. I wrote, illustrated, designed and laid it out from start to finish.

Piece

Printed book with original text and illustrations.

The project

The story follows the stages of grief. Each chapter is a different emotional state, from anger to acceptance, and the text and illustrations move forward together so you can see the change as well as read it. At the end the butterfly appears, and closing a chapter stops being just a loss and becomes the start of a change.
